# Official multi-assay graph prediction with matched neighborhoods

This plan was fixed before viewing new validation or test scores. Use the full official ogbg-molpcba v1 scaffold split. The primary endpoint is the official macro average precision (AP): restrict each assay to observed labels, retain assays with both positive and negative labels, and average their sklearn average precisions equally. Report the mean metric over five fits, not the performance of an ensemble. No assay, seed or test molecule is selected by outcome. This benchmark is larger than the preceding single-property study, but the precision for a paired difference will be estimated rather than presumed.

Use S, symmetric normalized undirected untyped adjacency; isolated nodes have zero inverse degree. Let p be the indicator for a noncarbon heavy atom. Inputs common to all arms are graph size, all OGB categorical atom and bond histograms, counts of directed one-hop neighbors by the six ordered element groups (C, N, O, halogens, P/S, other), six-group sums of static diagonal orders 2–6 and six-group sums of both Local and Spatial shifted diagonals through order four (the second orders coincide). Scale group sums and count histograms by sqrt(n). Mean, population SD and maximum of each common static order over eligible carbon roots (no heavy noncarbon one-hop neighbor), and fraction eligible, also enter every head. Root-local uses (S+p_i I)/2 and Spatial uses (S+Diag(p))/2. The only varying features are 18 summaries of orders five and six: the two sums for each of the six element groups, and their mean, SD and maximum over eligible carbon roots. A vanished eligible group gives zero statistics. Omit coordinates and any identity/split/scaffold labels. Check the order-five two-hop identity on all eligible roots. Pad Common with 18 fixed zeros so its size, optimization budget and initialization match Local and Spatial.

Train one 256/128 hidden-unit ReLU MLP per arm and seed, with dropout .10 and a 128-output layer. Five paired seeds 103,211,307,401,503 use identical initialization, batches and dropout draws between arms. Standardize on training data, SD floor 1e-5, clip at +/-8. Minibatch 2048, 20 epochs, AdamW lr .001 decay .0001. Mask missing labels in binary cross entropy; per-assay training positive weights are sqrt(negative/positive), clipped to [1,20]. Select each fit's earliest maximum validation macro AP among epochs 4,8,12,16,20. Fit all validation-selected models before evaluating test metrics. Use FP64 graph powers and FP32 neural fitting on an NVIDIA H100. Data, per-example predictions, per-assay outcomes and checkpoints stay on the server.
