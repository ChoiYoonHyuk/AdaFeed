# Context-gated fingerprint readout on official ogbg-molpcba

This protocol is fixed before training or evaluation of this new representation. The official scaffold test set has been used by the earlier study. This is a new matched follow-up on the same benchmark, not an independently held-out dataset.

Primary outcome is the official OGB equal-weight macro average precision across eligible assays. All 437,929 molecules use the published splits and all known labels. The three arms share an unsigned radius-two 2,048-bit Morgan fingerprint with chirality (presence, RDKit), the original 300 common graph descriptors, and one-hop environment counts and static/low-order returns. Hashed environments use the nine OGB atom fields and an unordered multiset of incident bond/neighbor signatures, mapped to a fixed 64-bin table. Every arm receives, within each environment, the count, static orders 2–6, root-local orders 2–4 and spatial orders 3–4 (11 columns, sums divided by square root of molecule size). The atom field is the noncarbon-heavy-atom indicator, as in the prior study. The additional two columns in each environment are zeros, root-local orders 5/6, or spatial orders 5/6. Standardize all continuous columns on the train split and clip at +/-8; fingerprints are unscaled binary inputs. No selection of atom types, assays, molecules, thresholds or subgroups uses assay outcomes.

The 512-unit shared trunk reads common inputs. Sixty-four sigmoid gates computed from its hidden state modulate the standardized pair of higher moments for their environment. The concatenated trunk and gated coordinates feed a 256-unit ReLU layer and 128 logits. Apply 0.15 dropout after each ReLU. AdamW learning rate .001 and weight decay .0001, 2,048 molecules per batch, masked BCE and square-root positive class weights clipped to [1,20]. Shuffle once per epoch. Use seeds 103, 211, 307, 401 and 503, same initialization/randomization and parameter count across arms. Select highest validation AP every four epochs (earliest tie), stop after eight unimproved checks, hard stop at 200 epochs. No arm-specific tuning. After all 15 validation choices are frozen, evaluate each once on the official test. Test molecule-group and scaffold-group delete-100 intervals and paired-seed t intervals follow revision16. Spatial superiority requires positive lower bounds for both its paired-seed and molecule intervals against each comparator. Report all three absolute results and both contrasts regardless of outcome. Compare revision17 absolute results descriptively only; fingerprinting, pooling and head all change. One optional validation-only seed-103 Common ablation zeroes the fingerprint using the same head and stopping rule. It does not influence the selection of the primary method and is never evaluated on test.
