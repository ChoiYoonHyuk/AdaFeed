# Fresh fixed-size training-randomness panel, CASCADE Exp5K

This fixed precision study was specified after seeing the original five-seed Exp5K test comparisons. The original paired fit interval for Root-local minus Spatial had half-width about 0.12 ppm. Sixty-four independent new paired training initializations target half-width about 0.025 ppm if the paired SD is 0.10 ppm. This is an approximate precision calculation, not a guarantee of a positive finding. Select the sample size once and evaluate all 64 pairs per contrast. The original five fits are not pooled with these new fits. No early look at new test scores, no additional runs or adaptive extension, no new data/targets/features/architectures and no tuning.

Use the exact original pinned 2D carbon-shift inputs, original authors' splits and training/validation/test cohort hashes at this location. Import the original model, scaler transformation, design, fitting function, optimizer, learning-rate reduction, early stopping, and scoring function unchanged. Commit SHA 4e6f5a9e50162c80cf2382397bdfa41334ff2e0d of patonlab/CASCADE; official 500 held-out molecules, 5156 measured carbon assignments. Common, Root-local and Spatial receive the original identical shared 320 channels, plus their respective 15 shift slots. Use identical initialization, minibatch and dropout seed pairing across arms. Each checkpoint is the earliest smallest validation MAE, maximum 300 epochs and validation patience 35. Use each model's original atom-weighted MAE in ppm and same standardized training target, same H100, same FP32/TF32 policies.

Generate 64 new seeds by numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(202611220518)).integers(1, 2**31-10001, size=64, dtype=int64), in draw order. Assert all 64 unique and disjoint from 103,211,307,401,503. This generator, range and order are frozen before fitting. Save individual validation-only checkpoints. Verify all 192 selection records and publish a digest of their checkpoints to a server-local manifest before any fresh test scores. Reuse only exact-hash-matching validation checkpoints after interruption. Aggregate reporting retains every seed's selected and stopping epoch, validation record and held-out MAE for all three arms. Do not treat repeated predictions from one test molecule as independent measurements.

Primary endpoint: five-thousand one-hundred fifty-six official held-out carbon shifts. Positive delta is reference MAE minus Spatial MAE. For each of Root-local and Common, report the fresh-panel paired seed t95 (63 df) and paired molecule percentile95 (2,000 multinomial resamples of the 500 held-out molecules, RNG 180425), always using the same sampled molecules for all fits and arms, pooling their atom errors by atom count. Declare joint superiority only if the four lower bounds are strictly positive. Report a crossed seed-by-molecule sensitivity with 10,000 draws from PCG64(180426): independently sample 64 seed indices and all represented test molecules with replacement per draw; hold the resampled indices fixed across the two controls and Spatial, average errors over seeds and pool molecule atom errors by their resampled sizes. Retain the primary criterion regardless of this sensitivity. Report original descriptive outputs of exact-distance-three and canonical-input deduplication, molecule macro means, per-seed values, caps and stopping epochs. For any descriptive subgroup the same statistics are conditional on its represented molecules; it cannot establish overall success. Variation from new initializations and repeat evaluation on the known published test split do not supply fresh held-out molecules. Release code, frozen protocol, cohort/checkpoint provenance digests and aggregate metrics only; keep atom-level data and checkpoints on the H100 server.
