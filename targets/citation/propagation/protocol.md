# Strong common propagation on the official ogbn-arxiv task

This design is frozen before fitting the new models or seeing their validation or test outcomes. The earlier ridge study on the same temporal split and its aggregate high-heterogeneity and overall accuracies were known when specifying this experiment. No new split, task or architecture is selected after evaluating this experiment.

Use the official temporal train/valid/test partition (90,941/29,799/48,603 papers; 40 subjects). Symmetrize the citation edges, binarize duplicates, and remove self-edges to define A. Each model sees the entire public graph and text covariates, including later papers, exclusively training labels during optimization and official validation accuracy for model selection. This is the standard transductive setting with a chronological label split. The primary endpoint is official accuracy averaged over the five separate models, not accuracy of a seed ensemble.

Common inputs are the public 128 text coordinates x and their first three propagated blocks T x, T² x, T³ x, where T is the symmetrically normalized A+I. Include log(1+degree) and r_i=(S²)_ii, where S is the symmetric normalized loop-free adjacency. Also include the same 128 ordinary neighbor means and population SDs of the transformed text field p=tanh((x−mean_train)/max(sd_train,1e−8)) coordinatewise. For each of the three arms append exactly 128 slots. Common gets zero. Root-local gets p_i. Spatial gets W_i=sum_{j~i} p_j/d_j divided by sum_{j~i} 1/d_j. At isolates both final blocks and ordinary neighbor summaries are zero. For nonisolates the difference W_i−p_i is exactly the difference between spatial and root-local cubic diagonals divided by r_i, separately in each field channel. The normalized extractor adds an inverse-neighbor-degree weighted average of nonlinear text fields. All three arms use identical graph and document information.

Use two hidden layers of width 256, each Linear, BatchNorm1d, ReLU, dropout .4, followed by a 40-class linear output. Standardize every feature coordinate using training means and population SDs with floor 1e−6. Zero common slots stay zero. Use no label propagation, no graph labels or supervision outside the train mask, and no class weighting. All five seeds (103, 211, 307, 401, 503) use paired model initialization, minibatch permutations, dropout RNG, batch size 2048, FP32 tensors with TF32 matrix multiplication allowed, AdamW lr .001, weight decay 1e−4, ordinary cross-entropy. Train for up to 120 epochs, select the earliest highest validation accuracy at the end of every epoch, and stop after 20 consecutive nonimprovements. No schedule, hyperparameter sweep, or test-based restarts. Select all 15 checkpoints before producing test scores.

Report accuracy for all arms and per-seed paired differences. Primary directional comparison requires the two-sided 95% seed t intervals (4 degrees of freedom) for BOTH Spatial−Local and Spatial−Common overall accuracy to have positive lower bounds. Summarize per-class macro recall over represented classes overall and in both predetermined strata for each arm. The stratifier is h_i, the coefficient of variation of reciprocal neighbor degrees (zero for degree<2), and upper stratum h_i strictly above the linearly interpolated training 75th percentile used by the previous ridge study, with all remaining nodes in the complement. Evaluate high-minus-complement interactions in paired test accuracy, and per-stratum accuracy, without refitting or threshold revision. Record both signs as observed. The seed intervals quantify optimization variability conditional on this one dependent citation graph. Store all raw data, intermediate features, individual predictions and model weights only on the H100 server. Export the fixed protocol, implementation and aggregate metrics.
