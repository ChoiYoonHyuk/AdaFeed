We measure the rootwise spatial-minus-local cubic difference, one scalar at every vertex, in FP64 on one H100. The observable, graph sizes, field and summaries below are fixed before timing. Read the official ogbn-arxiv graph on the server, remove loops, symmetrize and collapse duplicates. Use the 1,024- and 4,096-node induced BFS prefixes at root 36138 used in the manuscript and the entire 169,343-node graph, including isolates. Normalize by endpoint degrees computed on each induced graph. Set r_i to the diagonal of S squared and p_i to 2r_i-1. Do not use prediction targets.

Compare the squared-edge CSR formula with ordinary column propagation evaluating the two cubic returns at each root. Both consume the resident graph and field. Credit the direct column implementation with common first step and shared application of S on the second step. Separate the two arms before the final inner product. Verify the two pooling identities on all vertices at all sizes. Time the squared-edge formula at all sizes, and both methods at the two prefix sizes. Do not extrapolate full direct or EPWL times.
